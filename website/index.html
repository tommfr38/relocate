Features

Everything for testing location-aware apps

Built for the way iOS developers and QA teams actually work — precise, fast, and honest about what's happening on the device.

Simulator & physical iPhone

Drive the iOS Simulator through CoreSimulator, or a cable-connected iPhone over Apple's trusted developer channel — same workspace, same controls.

Search & precise coordinates

Type an address, drop a pin on the map, or enter exact latitude and longitude — whichever fits the test case.

Timed routes & GPX

Build multi-waypoint routes with speed control, or import and export GPX files to replay real-world tracks on the device.

Saved places

Keep the coordinates you test against most — rename, reorder, or jump back to any of them in a click.

Speed-controlled playback

Walk, cycle, drive, or push past highway speed — routes play back at exactly the pace your test needs, in km/h.

Guided iPhone setup

A built-in step-by-step tutorial covers cable trust, Developer Mode, and everything else needed before the first session.

How it works

From cable to coordinates in three steps

1

Connect and trust

Plug the iPhone in with a data cable, unlock it, and tap Trust. Relocate never simulates location over Wi-Fi.

2

Pick a location or route

Search, drop a pin, enter coordinates, or string together waypoints into a timed route.

3

Simulate, then restore

Press Set Location to move the device there. Press Stop at any point to hand control back to the real GPS.
